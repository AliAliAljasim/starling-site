# Starling privacy policy

_Last updated: 25 November 2026_

Starling is a desktop video studio for one creator's own channel. It runs entirely on your computer. There is no
Starling server, no account with us, and no analytics or tracking.

## What Starling accesses

When you connect YouTube, you sign in with Google and allow Starling to upload videos to your channel
(`youtube.upload`) and to read your channel's name (`youtube.readonly`). Starling uses this only to:

- show which channel is connected;
- upload a video you exported, with the title, description and tags you wrote, when you click **Post**;
- set that video's thumbnail when you made one.

Starling does not read, change or delete your other videos, playlists, comments or subscribers.

When you connect Facebook and Instagram, Starling uses your Facebook Page and its linked Instagram account only to
publish the video and caption you chose when you click **Post**.

## Where your data goes

Starling talks directly from your computer to Google's and Meta's servers. Nothing is sent to the developer or to
anyone else. Your sign-in tokens and app keys are stored only on your computer, in a file that only your user account
can read. Your videos, notes and settings also stay on your computer.

## Removing access

Click **Disconnect** in Starling's Settings → Accounts, and remove Starling's access at
https://myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) or in Facebook's Settings → Business integrations (Meta).
Uninstalling Starling removes the app; delete `~/.config/zumo/accounts.json` in WSL to remove the saved tokens.

## Google API Services

Starling's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements. See also the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
